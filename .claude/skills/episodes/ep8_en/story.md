## Script

1. Did you know every hot dog you put in your mouth gets stripped naked first?
2. Relax. Today I'll show you why.
3. Crushed ice, a skin that isn't meat, a 155-degree oven… and a secret fourth step.
4. A hot dog is a long, tender tube of finely ground meat, made for a bun.
5. But what happens before it reaches your home?
6. Step one: meat chopped with crushed ice into a paste.
7. The fat goes in last, ice cold: if it warms up, the hot dog sweats fat.
8. Step two: the paste gets pushed under pressure into a cellulose casing.
9. And here, size does matter: the casing decides how thick it gets.
10. A machine twists it, and out come the links.
11. Step three: the oven, to 155 degrees inside, then a cold shower.
12. Look at what someone commented:
13. Wait… do we eat that skin?
14. Nope. Cellulose doesn't digest. Hence, step four.
15. Steam, a blade slits it open, and a jet of air rips it off.
16. Like peeling the plastic off a brand-new phone.
17. As it cooks, the outer meat sets into its own skin.
18. Erwin Freund figured that out in the 1920s… by accident.
19. Before: a paste full of ice. Now: a naked hot dog with your friend's name on it.
