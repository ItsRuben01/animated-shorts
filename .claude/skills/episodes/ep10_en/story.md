## Script

1. Did you know you've been chewing almost the same rubber as a tire your whole life?
2. Relax. Today I'll show you how it's made.
3. Tire rubber, a giant mixer, 50 degrees… and a fourth step that gives it protection.
4. Gum is rubber you chew, but never swallow.
5. But what is it made of?
6. The Maya chewed the sap of a tree: the sapodilla.
7. Today it's butyl rubber, like a tire's inner tube.
8. Its chains tangle like spaghetti. That's why it stretches.
9. Step one: the gum base melts in the mixer.
10. Sugar, syrup and flavor: five to fifteen minutes of mixing.
11. One of the few things that goes in hard… and comes out soft.
12. Step two: an extruder pushes it out at about 50 degrees Celsius, and rollers flatten it.
13. Step three: it's cut and left to cool.
14. Look at what someone commented:
15. If I swallow it, does it stay inside me for seven years?
16. Myth. It passes in a few days, almost whole.
17. Step four: coating. Spinning drums add syrup, layer by layer.
18. Like painting a wall: lots of thin coats.
19. Careful: if the air goes over 8 percent humidity, the shell gets sticky.
20. In the end, the shell is a third of the gum's weight.
21. Before: tree sap. Now: a coated piece with your friend's name on it.
22. Tag or send this video to your friend who never lets go of their rubber.
23. See you next time! Hit like and follow for more breakdowns like this.
