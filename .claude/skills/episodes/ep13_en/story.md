## Guion

1. Your bananas show up green… and get locked in a room full of gas.
2. Relax: it's how they turn yellow.
3. A ship at 13 degrees, a sealed room, an invisible gas… and a trick for your kitchen.
4. They're picked green and cross the ocean at about 13 degrees.
5. Not one lower: below that, the peel turns grey and they won't ripen right.
6. On arrival, into the ripening room: door shut, ethylene in.
7. Just a whiff: 100 to 150 parts per million, for a day or two.
8. Someone asked: gas on my fruit? Isn't that weird chemistry?
9. It's the same hormone the banana makes to ripen. They just give it a little nudge.
10. The kitchen trick: a paper bag, plus one apple.
11. Tag your friend who always eats them green.
12. See you next time! Hit like and follow for more.
