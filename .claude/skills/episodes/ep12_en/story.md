## Guion (una voz por plano)

1. Nobody cuts this screw's thread… it's pressed in.
2. Goes in smooth… comes out threaded.
3. Wire, hammer blows, pressure… and a fourth detail that makes it stronger.
4. It starts as steel wire: a blade chops it into little blanks.
5. Then, cold, it gets punched until the end swells into a head.
6. Now the thread: it rolls between two steel dies that squeeze it.
7. One pass… up to 500 a minute. Nobody gets tired here.
8. Someone asked: why not just cut the thread, like always?
9. Cutting removes steel and slices its fibers. Squeezing makes them follow the thread.
10. That's the fourth detail: up to 30 % stronger, without losing a single gram.
11. Tag your friend who over-tightens absolutely everything.
12. See you next time! Hit like and follow for more.
