## Script (one voice per shot)

1. Do you love putting strangers' cucumbers in your mouth?
2. Don't worry: today I've got the solution to enjoy them even more.
3. High-pressure water, 6 % salt, weeks in a tank… and a fourth step that keeps it from being dangerous.
4. A cucumber is a veeery long, green vegetable that people love putting whole in their mouth.
5. But what really happens before it reaches your home?

9. For weeks, bacteria turn their sugar into acid, all the way to the center.
10. But they also release gas, and the pickle can go hollow inside.
11. So they bubble nitrogen through to sweep the gas out.

17. Below pH 4.6, botulism can't grow.
18. Before, a cucumber from the field; now, a sealed jar with your friend's name on it.
